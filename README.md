## 🎨 Our Product Lines
- **Ordinary Non-leafing Aluminum Paste**  
  Bright, fine particle size, high hiding power; used for industrial coatings, marine paint, hammer paint, and mechanical primers.

- **Leafing Aluminum Paste**  
  Good oxidation resistance, strong floating ability, excellent shielding and protection; used for anti-corrosion coatings, tank/ship coatings, aerosols, and leather finishes.

- **High-leafing Aluminum Paste**  
  Smooth surface, neat orientation, high whiteness, strong mirror & chrome effect; for high-grade inks, automotive wheels, plastics, and hardware coatings.

- **Imitation Plating Aluminum Paste (Non-leafing)**  
  Fine and uniform particle, strong metallic effect, high brightness & whiteness; for high-end appliances, hardware, car refinish, plastic paint, and decoration.

- **Fine White Series Aluminum Paste (Non-leafing)**  
  High hiding power, bright white metallic effect, good flatness; for plastics, electronics, coil coatings, hardware, and appliances.

- **Bright Aluminum Paste (Non-leafing)**  
  High smoothness, strong metallic luster, good hiding power; for high-end appliances, hardware, car repair paint, and fine decoration.

- **High Bright Sparkle Aluminum Paste (Non-leafing)**  
  “Silver dollar” shape, high flash, good leveling, heat & acid resistance; for hardware, appliances, crafts, plastics, and automotive refinish.

- **VMP (Vacuum Metallized Pigments)**  
  High-purity aluminum, excellent mirror chrome effect, high reflectivity; for car paint, wheel coatings, spray paint, inks, and nail polish.

- **Water-based Aluminum Paste**  
  Silica or modified polyester treated, good water dispersibility, low VOC, acid & alkali resistance; for waterborne coatings and inks.

- **Balloon-specific Water-based Aluminum Paste**  
  Specially developed for metallic latex balloons; excellent dispersion, high alkali resistance, stable, and brilliant metallic effect.

- **Resin-coated Aluminum Paste**  
  Polymer-coated flakes, outstanding weatherability, corrosion resistance, adhesion; for high-end coatings: home appliances, mobile phones, coil, and exterior paints.

- **Masterbatch-grade Aluminum Paste**  
  Special heat-resistant formulation, excellent processing stability, uniform dispersion; specially designed for plastic color masterbatch.

- **Aluminum Paste for AAC**  
  High activity, good water dispersibility, stable performance; used as foaming agent for aerated concrete blocks.
